My path to IT

Mid-high school was wild. Deciding careers was exhilarating yet confusing, emotional for sure, and honestly stressful.

Would you believe IT was my third choice, after Law and Psychology?

Yeah. Third.

But somehow that third choice turned into my first.

I still remember my first line of code. Executing it was oddly heart warming. That feeling of creating something out of nothing, those two words that formed a sentence that looked so basic, that's where it all started.

And yes… bugs became part of the journey.

The kind that show up right before sleep and you realise that you ended a line with a colon instead of a semi-colon. Those moments became my daily companions.
